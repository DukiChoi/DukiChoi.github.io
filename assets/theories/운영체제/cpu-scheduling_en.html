Three ways to choose the next process

FCFS runs processes to completion in arrival order. It is simple, but a long process at the front makes short processes wait.

SJF chooses the shortest burst among processes that have arrived. It can reduce average waiting time, but burst lengths are difficult to predict and long jobs may starve if short jobs keep arriving.

Round robin gives each process a fixed time slice and then moves it to the back of the queue. Processes take turns, but a very small quantum increases context-switch overhead.

Calculating waiting time

Add all time spent ready but waiting for the CPU.

Waiting time = completion time − arrival time − burst time
